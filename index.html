Common questions

How do I add an article? In Safari or another app, tap Share and choose margin. The article appears when you next open margin. You can also paste a link on margin’s home screen.

How do I ask a question? In audio mode, tap the waveform button and ask. After an answer, margin listens for a few seconds, so you can ask a follow-up without tapping again. To ask hands-free, turn on Say “Marigold” to ask in Settings, change the word in Settings → Wake word if you like, and say it before your question.

Can the reader control playback? Yes. Ask it to rewind a minute, go back to a part of the article, start over, read faster or slower, pause, set a sleep timer, or highlight what you just heard.

Why does the voice take a moment to start? The first audio for a new article is made when you press Play. With Download whole article on in Settings, the rest is ready by the time you need it.

How do I listen to several articles in a row? Long-press an article and choose Add to queue, then press Play next to Up next.

How do I report a bad answer? Press and hold the answer and choose Report answer.

Can I use margin without AI services? Yes. Turn off Settings → Privacy → Use AI services. Reading, highlights, and notes keep working.
