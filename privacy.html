margin – listen & ask

Privacy policy

Effective October 14, 2026

In short

  • margin has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. We do not sell or share your data for advertising.
  • Your saved articles, highlights, notes, and questions are stored on your iPhone.
  • To read articles aloud, answer your questions, and pick articles for you, margin sends some text to AI services: our own voice server, and Groq, Inc., which runs the gpt-oss model for answers and Discover. It does this only after you agree, and you can turn it off in Settings.
  • margin never sends or keeps recordings of your voice. Speech becomes text on your iPhone.

What stays on your iPhone

margin stores the following on your device only:

  • The articles you save: their text, images, and links.
  • Your reading and listening position, highlights, notes, your questions and the answers, and your listening queue.
  • Narration audio that margin saves so articles play without waiting or offline.
  • Your settings, and an OpenAI key if you add one, which is kept in the iPhone Keychain.

We do not receive a copy of any of this. Deleting an article in margin deletes its saved data, and deleting the app deletes everything.

Microphone and speech recognition

margin uses the microphone to hear your questions about the article you are listening to:

  • When you tap the ask button in audio mode, margin listens until you finish your question.
  • After an answer, margin listens for about 5 more seconds, so you can ask a follow-up question without tapping again.
  • Wake word (off unless you turn it on). In Settings, the switch Say “Marigold” to ask lets margin listen for a word you choose. While it is on and an article plays, margin listens for that word on your iPhone, also when margin is in the background or your phone is locked. iOS shows that the microphone is in use. When you pause in the background, margin turns the microphone off.

Speech is turned into text on your iPhone by Apple’s speech recognition. margin does not record or keep audio of your voice, and never sends audio to us or to anyone else. Only the text of a question you ask goes to the answer service described below. Speech before the wake word is not sent. When you teach margin your wake word in Settings, it keeps only the text spellings it heard, on your iPhone.

AI services

Before the first time any text is sent, margin explains this and asks for your permission. If you do not agree, margin only reads articles and sends nothing. You can change your choice in Settings → Privacy → Use AI services.

  • Voice (Kokoro). To make the narration you hear, the text of the article is sent to a speech server that we run with a cloud provider. The text is used to create the audio and is not kept afterwards.
  • Answers (gpt-oss, provided by Groq, Inc.). When you ask a question, margin sends the text of your question, the article text around where you paused (up to about 40,000 characters), and earlier questions and answers from that session to Groq, Inc., which runs the gpt-oss model.
  • Discover picks (gpt-oss, provided by Groq, Inc.). To pick new articles for you, margin sends the titles and website names of your recently saved articles, a few of your recent questions, and the titles of candidate stories to Groq.
  • OpenAI (only if you choose it). If you choose OpenAI for the voice or answers in Settings and add your own OpenAI key, the same kinds of text go to OpenAI under your own OpenAI account.

In the app, the consent screen and Settings name these services by their models: Kokoro for the voice, and gpt-oss for answers and Discover. The company that receives the text for gpt-oss is Groq, Inc.

These services process the text to provide the feature you asked for. Their handling of it is also covered by their own privacy policies, linked above. margin does not send your name, contact details, or device identifiers with these requests.

Reporting an answer

If an answer is wrong or harmful, press and hold it and choose Report answer. Your Mail app opens with an email to us that contains the question and the answer. Nothing is sent until you send that email.

Websites and feeds

When you add an article, margin downloads the page directly from that website, as a browser would. Discover downloads public stories from Hacker News and from feeds you choose to follow. Those websites can see a normal web request from your device. Links in articles open in Safari inside margin.

Analytics and crash reports

margin does not include analytics or advertising tools. If you have chosen to share diagnostics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports. TestFlight may share feedback and screenshots you choose to send.

Children

margin is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be described in the app.
